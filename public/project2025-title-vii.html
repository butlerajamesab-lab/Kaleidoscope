Employment discrimination and gender identity

The basic idea

What is changing?

A legal right and a way to enforce it are not the same thing.

The represented federal court protection remains while some federal agency guidance or enforcement pathways change. Iowa removes the represented state protection and also restricts local governments from restoring broader protected categories.

Three terms worth knowing

Title VII
A federal employment-discrimination law.
EEOC
U.S. Equal Employment Opportunity Commission — the federal agency responsible for enforcing federal workplace-discrimination laws.
Preemption
A state rule that limits what local governments are allowed to regulate or protect.

Why the distinction matters

A federal court right, an agency enforcement path, a private lawsuit, a state protection, and a city or county protection are different layers. One layer can remain while another becomes narrower or disappears.
