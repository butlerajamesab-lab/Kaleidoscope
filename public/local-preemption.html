The basic idea

What is being compared?

Similar legal techniques do not prove coordination.

The source record identifies a family of state laws or legal strategies that can restrict local governments from maintaining broader nondiscrimination protections. Kaleidoscope compares their structure and timing without assuming they were centrally coordinated.

One legal term worth knowing

Preemption
A state rule that limits what local governments are allowed to regulate or protect.

Why the distinction matters

A state can use a similar legal technique at a different time, through a different law, and with a different scope. Kaleidoscope keeps those differences visible instead of treating membership in the same family as proof of a common cause or organizer.
